What I changed from the mockup

  • No Silver. That's a UK BPI level, and the RIAA doesn't have one.
  • "Units earned" or "eligible" instead of "RIAA Certified." Certification needs an audit, and a screenshot claiming it without one could get an artist, or Mogul, into trouble.
  • Generic medals with no RIAA logo. The RIAA protects its trademarks.

How sharing would work

  • Download PNG. Rendered in the browser at 1080 px, ready for Instagram, TikTok or X.
  • Share button. On phones it opens the share sheet with the image attached.
  • Share link. A public page for each artist with a preview image, so pasting the link into X, iMessage or LinkedIn shows the card.
