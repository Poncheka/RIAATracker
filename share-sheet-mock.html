Card first

The card is centered and is the biggest thing on screen. Switch with the tabs, the arrows, or a swipe on mobile. The song picker only appears on Countdown.

Neutral tab names

"Celebrate" instead of "I went Gold!", so the tab doesn't contradict a card that says Platinum. If nothing's certified yet, Celebrate is hidden and the sheet opens on Countdown.

Straight to socials

Instagram and TikTok can't take a post from a website, so those buttons hand the image to the phone's share sheet. X, Facebook and LinkedIn post your link, and the card shows as its preview.
