A Gold single takes **75 million on-demand US streams**. A Gold album takes **750 million**. Those are the numbers if streams are all you have. Paid downloads close the gap faster, and anything played outside the US doesn't count at all.

## The short version

The RIAA certifies in units, not streams. Every certification level is a unit count, and streams convert into units at a fixed rate.

| Level | Units | Single: US streams | Album: US streams |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold | 500,000 | 75,000,000 | 750,000,000 |
| Platinum | 1,000,000 | 150,000,000 | 1,500,000,000 |
| 2× Platinum | 2,000,000 | 300,000,000 | 3,000,000,000 |
| Diamond | 10,000,000 | 1,500,000,000 | 15,000,000,000 |

After Platinum, every additional million units is another multi-Platinum level.

## How streams turn into units

**Singles:** 150 on-demand streams = 1 unit. One paid permanent download = 1 unit.

**Albums:** 1,500 on-demand streams = 1 unit. So do 10 track downloads, or 1 album download. Every track on the album counts toward it, including singles you released before the album came out. [More on album units](/guides/riaa-album-units-explained/).

So a single with 60 million US streams and 100,000 iTunes downloads sits at 400,000 + 100,000 = 500,000 units. That's Gold.

## Why your Spotify number is probably higher than your RIAA number

The total on your Spotify for Artists dashboard is worldwide. The RIAA only counts the United States. For a lot of independent artists, the US is a third or less of total plays.

The counter also leaves out:

- Plays on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and other user-generated content
- YouTube Content ID claims on other people's videos (official videos on your channel do count)
- Radio-style plays where the listener didn't pick the song, like SiriusXM or non-interactive Pandora
- Free promotional downloads

[Full list of what counts](/guides/what-counts-toward-riaa-certification/).

## How long does it take?

That depends on your US pace. A song doing 2 million US streams a month adds about 13,300 units a month, so it needs roughly three years to reach Gold. At 10 million a month it gets there in a little over seven months.
