Applying for RIAA certification takes a few hours of paperwork and a fee. Here's the whole process, start to finish.

## 1. Make sure you've actually crossed the line

Certification isn't automatic, and the fee isn't refunded if you fall short. Before anything else, total your US on-demand streams and paid downloads, after cutting out international plays, user-generated content and radio. [Here's what counts](/guides/what-counts-toward-riaa-certification/).

Where's My Plaque? does this from your statements and flags every song and album that has already passed a level.

## 2. Check it isn't already certified

Search the RIAA's Gold & Platinum database at riaa.com. Sometimes a label or co-owner already filed.

## 3. Pull your US sales summary

The auditor wants a report taken directly from the sales system, showing:

- US only
- On-demand streams and paid permanent downloads only, no user-generated content
- Broken out by ISRC (singles) or UPC (albums), store and format
- Release information and dates

If you send it as a spreadsheet, include a screenshot of the system it came from. Your distributor's statements or detailed artist royalty statements can back it up.

Where's My Plaque? builds this summary for any song that qualifies. Under **Plaques you've earned**, hit **Download → RIAA application package** and you get US sales by store, US sales by month and store, the ISRC list and your original statements in one zip.

## 4. Prepare label copy

A one-sheet for each ISRC or UPC: artist, title, version description, product number, runtime and credits. For singles, include every version you're combining (clean, explicit, radio edit).

## 5. Submit and pay

Submit through the RIAA's Quick Request Portal at certification.riaa.com. The fee is $450 per level for non-members and $350 per title for members.

## 6. Answer the auditor

Gelfand, Rennert & Feldman (GR&F) reviews the request for the RIAA. All documents are due within 30 days of your application. They may ask for audio files, DSP statements or more detail.

## 7. Order your plaque

Once it's certified, any artist, label or contributor on the title can order an official plaque from an RIAA-licensed framer. [How plaques work](/guides/how-to-get-an-official-riaa-plaque/).
