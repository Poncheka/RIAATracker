Once a title is certified, the plaque is something you buy. The RIAA doesn't mail one out.

## Who can order

Any artist, label or affiliated party who contributed to a certified title can buy an official award. Producers, writers, engineers and managers order them too. The RIAA doesn't limit how many get made.

## Where to order

Only RIAA-licensed framers can make official awards. The RIAA lists:

- Dejay Gold (Seattle, WA)
- Gold Rush Vinyl (Austin, TX)
- Jewel Box Platinum (Inglewood, CA)
- Metro Pro LLC (Pine Brook, NJ)
- The Award Group (NY, NJ, CT, DC, VA, FL)

The framer handles getting the label's authorization for you. Prices vary by framer and design, so ask each one for a quote.

## How to spot a real one

Official RIAA awards carry an RIAA hologram. Plenty of shops sell "Gold record" plaques with no certification behind them. They're decoration, not an award.

## No certification yet?

The plaque comes last. First the title has to cross 500,000 US units, and then someone has to [apply and pass the audit](/guides/how-to-apply-for-riaa-certification/).
