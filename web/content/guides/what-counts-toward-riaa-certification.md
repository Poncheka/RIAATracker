Only two things count toward RIAA Gold and Platinum: **on-demand streams** and **paid downloads**, both **in the United States**. Everything else is left out, even when it pays you.

## Counts

- **On-demand audio streams** on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music, Tidal, Deezer and other authorized services, on paid or ad-supported tiers. 150 = 1 single unit. 1,500 = 1 album unit.
- **On-demand video streams** of official videos: your artist channel, your label's channel, or third-party channels where the label fully monetizes the video and it isn't flagged as user-generated.
- **Paid permanent downloads** from iTunes, Amazon and other legitimate stores, priced at market rate. 1 track download = 1 single unit. For albums, 10 track downloads = 1 unit, and 1 album download = 1 unit.
- **US military exchange (PX) sales.** These count as US.

## Doesn't count

- **Anything outside the US.** A stream in Toronto or London is worth nothing toward an RIAA award.
- **User-generated content.** TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook, Snapchat, YouTube Shorts using your sound, and YouTube Content ID claims on other people's uploads.
- **Radio-style plays where the listener didn't pick the song.** SiriusXM, non-interactive Pandora stations, and royalties collected by SoundExchange.
- **Free or promotional downloads,** including free-single promotions.
- **Cloud lockers and matching services** like iTunes Match. That's storage, not a sale.
- **Publishing and sync income.** Songwriter royalties, MLC mechanicals, PRO performances and sync fees are paid on the composition, not the recording.
- **Pre-orders,** until the release is out and the buyer has received it.

## Versions you can combine

For a single, you can count these together:

- Clean and explicit versions
- Radio edits and album versions
- Remixes no more than 90 seconds longer than the original

Live versions can't be combined with the studio track. They're certified separately.

For an album, alternate versions (remixes, instrumentals, live takes) only count if they appear on some version of the album, like a deluxe edition.

## Features and collaborations

A duet counts toward an artist's album only when that artist is a primary artist on the track. If you're credited as a featured guest, that song won't count toward your album.
