How close are you to Gold?

Connect your label or distributor and see every song and album's distance to RIAA Gold, Platinum and Diamond, counted the way the RIAA counts it: US on-demand streams and paid downloads only. Free and read-only.

How we count

  • Single: 150 US on-demand streams = 1 unit. 1 paid download = 1 unit. Gold = 500,000 units (75 million US streams).
  • Album: 1,500 US streams, 10 track downloads or 1 album download = 1 unit. Every track counts, including singles released first.
  • Left out: international plays, TikTok and other user-generated content, YouTube Content ID, radio-style plays, free downloads.
